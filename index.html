How to Play

  1. Emma starts. Flick the cartoon Willy to spin. The Willy and wheel spin in opposite directions.
  2. The wheel lands on Emma, Jim, Both or Bust.
  3. Every Emma, Jim and Both challenge has a 30, 45 or 60 second limit. The countdown starts immediately when the action screen appears, with a mechanical tick-tock that speeds up as time runs down. Take-turns challenges switch players automatically, restart the ticking and play the final fanfare only after both turns.
  4. Emma/Jim: that person does the card and earns 1 unbanked inch if completed. If the card belongs to the other player, the turn passes to them after completion.
  5. Both: both do the card, both earn 1 unbanked inch, and the current player keeps the turn.
  6. A completed card is removed for the rest of that game. A card marked Not Completed stays available, but it cannot appear again on the very next draw from that deck.
  7. Press Bank to save the active player’s unbanked inches. Banking ends the turn and play passes to the other player.
  8. Bust: if there are no unbanked inches, 0 inches are gained; otherwise all unbanked inches are lost. Play then swaps to the other player.
  9. The Willy gets longer as the active player builds their score. At 6 inches it reaches the outside of the wheel.
  10. The first player to bank 7 inches wins.
  11. When someone reaches 7 inches, tap the bean on the congratulations screen. The position names spin through the opening, slow down and stop on your winning scene.
  12. The winner music starts as soon as the congratulations screen appears and stops when Play Again is pressed.
  13. Tap the VS between the score panels at any time to restart the game.
