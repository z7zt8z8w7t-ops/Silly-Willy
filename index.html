How to Play

  1. Emma starts. Flick the cartoon Willy to spin. The Willy and wheel spin in opposite directions.
  2. The wheel lands on Emma, Jim, Both or Bust.
  3. Emma/Jim: that person does the card and earns 1 unbanked inch if completed. If the card belongs to the other player, the turn passes to them after completion.
  4. Both: both do the card, both earn 1 unbanked inch, and the current player keeps the turn.
  5. Press Bank to save the active player’s unbanked inches. Banking ends the turn and play passes to the other player.
  6. Bust: if there are no unbanked inches, 0 inches are gained; otherwise all unbanked inches are lost. Play then swaps to the other player.
  7. The Willy gets longer as the active player builds their score. At 6 inches it reaches the outside of the wheel.
  8. The first player to bank 7 inches wins.
  9. On the winner screen, tap Claim Your Prize and flick the Willy again to spin the full-size Prize Wheel. The position names read from the outside edge toward the centre, and the winning position becomes the prize.
  10. The winner music keeps playing through the winner and prize screens and stops when Play Again is pressed.
  11. Tap the VS between the score panels at any time to restart the game.
